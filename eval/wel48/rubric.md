# WEL-48 offline evaluation rubric

The frozen manifest defines six live records from three publishers and two complete held-outs.
F1 and F2 are fixtures, are always reported with the word **fixture**, and never contribute to a
live count. Completeness is computed by deterministic P1–P20 checks; model self-ratings are ignored.
Unsupported spans, unknown reasons, conflicts, and completeness are reported for every live row.
Raw publisher HTML remains outside git and carries no publication or reuse grant.
